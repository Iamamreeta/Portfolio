About Me

More than a resume Here's what drives me.

I'm studying Computer Engineering. Recruiting people , onboarding them, training them, and building the structures that let a community grow without losing what makes it feel human.

Over the last year I've led recruitment and onboarding for a 150+ member tech community, written the guidelines that shape how new members are welcomed in, and designed the retention strategies that keep people engaged after the first excitement fades. I've trained hundreds of students and young leaders, and I've watched communities scale from a handful of clubs to a nationwide network of thousands.

Technology gave me a way to think in systems. People and leadership are where I've chosen to spend that thinking. That's the direction I want my career to go.

I genuinely enjoy working with people — recruiting them, listening to them, helping them find their footing.

I've built and led teams, from 20-club zonal networks to a 150+ member community.

I create the structure — guidelines, evaluations, onboarding flows — that helps teams run smoothly.

I've trained and mentored 450+ people across leadership, digital literacy, and coding workshops.

My work has connected communities across Pokhara, Baglung, Damauli, Gorkha, and nationwide Nepal.
